## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
For each age group we look at the top finishing times in earlier years of the same race, counting recent years and bigger groups more, and adjust for how many runners usually show up. Small age groups also lean on the race's overall winning times, scaled for age. The predictions assume a normal day; a hot race or a new course can move them a lot.
