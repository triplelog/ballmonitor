## 1. What an MLTT rating point is worth

SPINDEX rating histories (spindex.mltt.com) cover 122 of 130 players: the rating each had before any day. For each player-season with at least 150 rallies (209), the edge fitted from that season's results alone, regressed on the rating the player had *before the season* (weighted by rallies): **1176 rating points per unit of rally edge**, average MLTT player at **2619** (correlation 0.54). No hindsight: the rating predates the results.

So 100 rating points is about 0.085 in edge: a 52.1% rally chance, a 57.8% chance to win a game.
