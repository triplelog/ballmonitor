# ncaaf_bowls: accuracy study

_Generated by `python -m ballmonitor.models.ncaaf_bowls.research`; do not edit by hand._

Real past seasons only (ESPN, 2016-2025; `research_data.py`). Every fitted number is tested walk-forward: fitted on the seasons before the test season. What is **not** tested here: which bowl a team goes to (the tie-in and bowl-pick part of the model), seeding, and the playoff games themselves beyond part 1's single-game check.

**Hindsight guard.** ESPN keeps one FPI snapshot per week. The snapshot labelled week N is the one after week N's games (the title-game week moves only the teams that played; 2023-2025 timestamps agree), so games of week N are predicted from snapshot N-1. The `last_updated` stamps of 2016-2022 are stale and are not used; 'week 1' snapshots (recomputed in December in some seasons) are dropped.

## 3. The committee: resume score vs. the real final rankings

**Fit.** Same formula as before; weights fitted as a Plackett-Luce ranking (the committee picks #1 from all teams, #2 from the rest, ...), in units of one rating point.
