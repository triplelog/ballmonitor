## Title
<!-- claude-ui:begin title -->
College football playoff & bowl odds
<!-- claude-ui:end title -->

## Summary (one sentence, shown under the title and on the home page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin summary -->
Every FBS team's chances to make the 12-team playoff, win the national title and land in each bowl.
<!-- claude-ui:end summary -->

## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
We play out the rest of the season thousands of times using ESPN's team ratings (FPI) and the real remaining schedule, then pick the conference champions, the 12-team playoff and every bowl the way the committees do. A team's chance is how often that happened.
<!-- claude-ui:end how -->
