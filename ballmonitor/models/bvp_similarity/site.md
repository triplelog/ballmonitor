## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
Each matchup starts from the hitter's and pitcher's own rates, adjusted for left- and right-handed splits, then blends in how hitters like him have done against pitchers like him and, a little, their actual head-to-head history. Checked against real matchups from the 2026 season, using only 2021–2025 data to predict them.
