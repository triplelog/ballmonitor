Polls and awards, re-counted

## Summary (one sentence, shown under the title and on the home page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin summary -->
What the AP Top 25 and the big MLB, NBA and NHL awards would look like if the same ballots were counted another way.
<!-- claude-ui:end summary -->

## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
We take every voter's published ballot and count it three ways: the official points, head-to-head (who more voters ranked higher in each pair) and instant runoff (drop the candidate with the fewest first-place votes and move those ballots on, round by round). Nothing about the ballots changes, only the counting.
<!-- claude-ui:end how -->
