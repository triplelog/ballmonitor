## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
Coaches are the best talent scouts, so a recruit's score comes from the schools that offered him a scholarship and lost: the more offers, and the stronger those schools, the higher the score. School strength is worked out from every recruiting battle in the class at once. Offer lists come from On3 and are incomplete for lower-rated recruits. The industry rank is On3's consensus of the four recruiting services' star ratings, for comparison.
