I am working on a relatively simple algorithm that ranks the recruiting classes of college football teams by using the data of which schools make offers. These rankings have similar performance to the standard star-based rankings with less opportunity for biased evaluations. Many fan bases believe their recruits are perpetually under appreciated, and this article would give a good idea of which schools really are favored by recruiting analysts.

I have scraped the offer database from Rivals (but this could easily be replaced with ESPN's database with presumably similar results). Then I treat each recruit's commitment as a victory over the other schools that made offers. I use a multi-competitor version of ELO to determine which schools are harder to beat. Then each recruit gets a score based on the quality of the losing schools. These scores are added up for all the commitments for a given school and the school with the most points is ranked number 1.
