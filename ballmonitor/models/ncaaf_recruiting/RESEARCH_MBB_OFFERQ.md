# MBB: offer quality from rivals' on-court strength, and ratings + offers

_Generated by `research_mbb_offerq.py` — do not edit by hand._

A recruit's **offer quality** is how strong (barttorvik adj. EM, seasons before his junior-year offers) the schools he turned down were — the committed school's own strength is never used. Recruit value = percentile(industry rating) + w × percentile(offer quality); class strength = decayed sum over commits.

1800 settings chosen on development classes 2014-2019 by the mean residual ρ (what the class adds beyond prior-season adj. EM and the consensus rank). Test classes 2020-2025 (6 independent class years, all D-I teams) played no part in any choice.
