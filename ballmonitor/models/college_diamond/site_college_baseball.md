## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
Every Division I team gets a rating from its scores, adjusted for who it played and where. We then play out the rest of the season thousands of times: conference tournaments, a selection committee that leans on the RPI, and the NCAA bracket. A team's chance is how often it got that far.
