## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
We start from each game's final margin, then adjust for how strong the opponent was and where the game was played, and pull small samples a little toward average. Comparing that with a benchmark team (a tournament bubble team in college, an average team in most pro leagues) gives the chance to beat it and the wins above it. Results come from ESPN and football-data.co.uk.
