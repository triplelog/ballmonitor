MLB bullpens: who pitches in relief

## Summary (one sentence, shown under the title and on the home page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin summary -->
Every team's relievers ranked by their chance to pitch, and how hard each bullpen worked in 2026.
<!-- claude-ui:end summary -->

## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
For each game we look at every reliever on the active roster: how often he has pitched lately, whether he has been closing or setting up, and whether he is tired from the last few days. We then play the game out 500 times, letting the manager make the calls real managers made in 2025, and count how often each reliever got in.
