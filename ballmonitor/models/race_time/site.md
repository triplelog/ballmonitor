## How this works (two or three plain sentences at the bottom of the page)
<!-- claude-ui:begin how -->
Longer races are slower per mile, and the model stretches a 5K time to each distance with a simple, well-known rule (women slow down a little less than men as races get longer). The ranges come from about 5,000 real road racers in Ohio whose later race times we compared with what their earlier races predicted.
<!-- claude-ui:end how -->
