Privacy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Ball Monitor has no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies, no ads and no analytics or tracking. We don't collect or sell personal information.

What your browser sends, and to whom

Like any website, loading a page sends your IP address and browser details to the servers that deliver it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs.
  • data.tabdn.com answers the live data tables (only on those pages): your filters and sorts are sent to it to fetch the matching rows.
  • jsDelivr supplies Python for the baseball charts, the first time you draw one. The formulas you type there are not sent anywhere; the chart is drawn in your browser.

Each of these services handles that information under its own privacy policy.

What is kept on your device

A few choices are saved in your browser's own storage so they are still there next time: which table columns you show, and your puzzle and game progress (Triple Play, Lineup, 27K). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Changes

If this changes (for example, if we add visitor counts), we'll update this page and the date above.
