About Ball Monitor

Ball Monitor turns sports data into plain tables and charts: college football playoff and bowl chances, recruiting rankings, college baseball and softball tournament odds, race-time predictions, and polls and awards re-counted with other voting methods. There are also a few daily baseball puzzles.

How the numbers are made

Every forecast comes from a small, open model: a few formulas and settings you can read and change. Each page ends with "How this works" in a few sentences, and links to the full method, every setting and its source, and the model's known weaknesses.

The numbers are worked out ahead of time, when the site is rebuilt (once a day during each sport's season), not in your browser. The date of the last rebuild is at the bottom of every page.

Where the data comes from

Some of the main sources:

  • College football ratings (FPI) and schedules: ESPN. Recruiting offer lists: On3.
  • AP poll ballots: collegepolltracker.com. Award ballots: the BBWAA, NBA and PHWA.
  • Big-league box scores: Retrosheet. Season statistics: the Lahman Baseball Database (SABR, CC BY-SA 3.0).
  • NFL quarterback games: nflverse (CC BY 4.0).

Each page's "How this works" and each data table name their own sources.

Not betting advice

These are estimates from simple models, for fun and curiosity. They can be wrong, and they are not betting, financial or any other advice.
