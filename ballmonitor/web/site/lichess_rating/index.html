How this works

A Lichess rating only changes when you play rated games, so the forecast has two parts: the chance you play again in that speed before the date, and how far your rating tends to move if you do. Both were learned from a sample of real players in the Lichess open database (blitz games, 2014-2016) and checked on other players' later games (2016-2017). Today's players, and the other speeds, may behave a little differently. Your log stays in this browser; the optional Lichess button asks lichess.org directly for your public ratings and games.
