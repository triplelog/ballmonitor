How this works

The forecast starts from your last 12 weeks, leans a little toward your last 4 and back toward your last 6 months, and adjusts for the time of year. The ranges come from the real ups and downs of about 36,000 Strava runners who had run a big-city marathon, in 2019; it was checked on other runners in early 2020, before the pandemic changed everyone's training. It knows only distance and time, not heart rate or races, so a change in how hard you train moves the pace as much as a change in fitness. The marathon time is a published formula (Tanda, 2011) that we could not check. Your log stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
