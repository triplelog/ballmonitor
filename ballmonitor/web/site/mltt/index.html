How this works

Each player gets a single rating: their chance of winning a rally against an average MLTT opponent, estimated from every rally since 2024 and anchored by their SPINDEX rating at the time. Using MLTT's exact rules—games to 11 with a golden point at 10-10, plus the Golden Game race to 21 and its head start—we turn those ratings into game and match win probabilities. "Luck" is the difference between the points a team actually won and what their rallies were worth. From there, we simulate the rest of the season thousands of times. Keep in mind that MLTT matches are tough to predict (favorites have won around 60% historically), so treat the odds as rough guides. Match results and SPINDEX ratings come from mltt.com.
