How this works

Every player gets one number: their chance of winning a rally against an average MLTT player, worked out from every rally since 2024 and pulled toward the MLTT rating they had at the time. MLTT's own rules (games to 11 with a golden point at 10-10, the Golden Game race to 21 and its head start) turn those numbers into the chance of winning each game and each match, exactly. Luck is the points a team won beyond what its rallies were worth. The rest of the season is then played out thousands of times. MLTT matches are hard to call: in past seasons the favorite won about 60% of the time, so treat the chances as rough. Results and ratings come from MLTT's results site and its SPINDEX ratings.
