Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Ball Monitor has no accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. We don't collect or sell personal information.

What your browser sends

Like any website, loading a page sends your IP address and basic browser info to the servers delivering it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs.
  • data.tabdn.com powers the searchable data tables. Only when you visit a table page are your filters and sorts sent there to fetch matching rows.
  • 27k.ballmonitor.com runs 27K multiplayer. Opening 27K checks once whether the server is online. If you join or start a multiplayer game, it stores a random player ID your browser makes, the display name you enter, and your game moves. Inactive games are deleted after a month.
  • jsDelivr delivers Python the first time you draw a baseball chart. Your formulas never leave your browser—the chart runs locally on your device.

Each of these services follows its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your browser saves a few things locally so you don't lose them between visits: which table columns you hide or show, whether you've drawn a baseball chart before (so future charts load automatically), and your game and puzzle progress in Triple Play, Lineup, 27K, and Home Turf (games and saved copies). None of this leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data erases it.
